How to Play — LyrIQ

LyrIQ challenges your music knowledge! Rearrange scrambled lyrics to form the correct line and earn points before running out of lives.

Gameplay

1. On the Start page, enter an artist and press Play.
2. Drag & drop the word chips to form the correct lyric line.
3. Click Submit to check your guess.
4. Use the Hint button if you’re stuck (limited supply).
5. Run out of tries and you lose a life; lose all lives and it’s game over.

Difficulty

Mode Lives Hints Tries Decoy Words Point Multiplier
Easy 3 5 3 No x1
Medium 3 3 2 Yes x2
Hard 1 3 1 Yes x5

Hints

Start with a limited number of hints (mode-dependent).
Reveals info like album, song title, or surrounding lyrics depending on difficulty.
Hints are shared across the whole game and do not reset between rounds.

Lives & Tries

Wrong guess lose a try.
Zero tries lose a life.
Zero lives game over.

Scoring

Points = 1 x (tries left) x (difficulty multiplier) on each correct lyric. Example: Medium mode (x2) with 2 tries left 4 points
